Task 2 · 范文精读（2篇）

常见主题与题型练习；原创参考范文，目标难度约 6.5–7，并非官方评分或考频排名。展开即可阅读，不影响作答草稿。

01 · 教育：在家教育（利弊比较型）

Question: In some countries, many parents educate their children at home instead of sending them to school. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

In some countries, an increasing number of parents choose to educate their children at home rather than send them to school. Although home education offers greater flexibility, I believe its disadvantages generally outweigh its advantages.

One major benefit of home education is that lessons can be adapted to the individual needs of each child. In a traditional classroom, teachers have to manage many students at the same time, which means that some children may struggle to keep up while others become bored. At home, parents can adjust the pace of learning and spend more time on difficult subjects. Moreover, a flexible schedule allows families to organise educational activities outside the classroom, such as visiting museums or conducting simple science experiments.

However, home education has several significant drawbacks. Firstly, children may have fewer opportunities to interact with people of their own age. School is not only a place to acquire knowledge but also an environment where young people learn to cooperate, resolve disagreements and develop friendships. These social skills are essential in adult life. Secondly, providing a high standard of education at home can be challenging. Most parents are not professionally trained teachers and may lack the knowledge or resources needed to teach certain subjects effectively. In addition, one parent may have to reduce their working hours, placing financial pressure on the family. While online courses and community activities can address some of these problems, they may not be equally accessible to every household.

In conclusion, despite the flexibility and personalised learning that home education can provide, I believe that the social, educational and financial benefits of attending school make it a better option for most children.

结构： 表明弊大于利 → 承认个性化学习的优势 → 论证社交、教学质量与经济成本 → 重申立场。

积累： be adapted to individual needs 适应个人需求；keep up 跟上进度；acquire knowledge 获取知识；resolve disagreements 解决分歧；place financial pressure on 给……带来经济压力。

逐段拆解 · 逻辑与句子功能

P1 · Introduction（改写题目 + 明确立场）

① In some countries ... school. — 改写题干，说明现象；② Although ... advantages. — 让步后明确弊大于利。不要只说双方都有道理。

P2 · Body 1（承认优势）

① One major benefit ... — 中心论点：个性化教学；② In a traditional classroom ... — 对比学校的局限；③ At home ... — 解释家庭教育如何解决问题；④ Moreover ... such as ... — 补充灵活性，并举博物馆和实验的例子。逻辑：优势 → 对比 → 解释 → 举例。

P3 · Body 2（重点论证劣势）

① However ... — 转折；② Firstly ... — 社交机会少；③ School is not only ... — 解释学校提供哪些社交技能；④ These social skills ... — 说明长期影响；⑤ Secondly ... — 教学质量问题；⑥ Most parents ... — 解释原因；⑦ In addition ... — 补充经济成本；⑧ While ... — 承认补救措施但指出可及性限制。逻辑：劣势 → 原因 → 后果 → 比较权衡。

P4 · Conclusion（重申立场）

In conclusion, despite ... — 承认优点，再综合社交、教育和经济因素，重申学校更适合多数儿童。不加入新论点。

可替换句型 · 绿色部分按题目更换

保留句法框架，替换绿色槽位；注意主谓一致、单复数及词性。例句不是万能套话。

开头：让步 + 立场

Although [现象] offers [优点], I believe its disadvantages generally outweigh its advantages.

换题示例： Although working from home offers greater flexibility, I believe its disadvantages generally outweigh its advantages.

虽然远程办公更灵活，但我认为弊大于利。offers 后接名词短语。
主体：提出优势

One major benefit of [名词/动名词] is that [完整句子].

换题示例： One major benefit of online learning is that students can study at their own pace.

网络学习的一大好处是学生能按自己的节奏学习。
解释：引出后果

[完整句子], which means that [完整句子].

换题示例： Public transport is limited in rural areas, which means that many residents rely on private cars.

农村公共交通有限，这意味着许多居民依赖私家车。
论证：不仅……而且……

[主语] is not only [名词短语] but also [名词短语].

换题示例： A workplace is not only a source of income but also an environment where people develop professional skills.

工作场所不仅是收入来源，也是培养职业技能的环境。两部分保持平行。
劣势：缺少机会

[人群] may have fewer opportunities to [动词原形].

换题示例： Remote workers may have fewer opportunities to communicate with their colleagues.

远程办公者与同事交流的机会可能更少。
劣势：缺乏资源

[人群] may lack the [资源/能力] needed to [动词原形].

换题示例： Small businesses may lack the financial resources needed to adopt new technologies.

小企业可能缺乏采用新技术所需的资金。
让步：承认补救措施

While [措施] can address some of these problems, [局限].

换题示例： While online courses can improve access to education, they may not meet the needs of every learner.

虽然网课能改善教育机会，但不一定满足所有学习者。
结尾：综合比较

In conclusion, despite [优点], I believe that [完整立场].

换题示例： In conclusion, despite the convenience of private cars, I believe that investing in public transport is more beneficial to society.

尽管私家车方便，我认为投资公共交通更有益于社会。despite 后接名词或动名词。
02 · 环境与消费：食物浪费（原因与对策型）

Question: People often throw away food bought from shops and restaurants. Why does this happen, and what could be done to reduce the amount of food wasted?

Food waste has become a serious problem in many societies, with large quantities of food purchased from shops and restaurants being thrown away. This problem is largely caused by poor purchasing habits and commercial practices, but several practical measures could help reduce it.

One reason why people waste food is that they often buy more than they actually need. Supermarkets frequently offer discounts on large quantities of products, encouraging customers to purchase food that they may not consume before it goes bad. In addition, many consumers fail to plan their meals in advance and forget about food stored in their refrigerators. Restaurants can also contribute to the problem by serving portions that are too large for customers to finish. As a result, perfectly edible food is sometimes discarded simply because people have bought or ordered too much.

There are several ways to address this issue. On an individual level, consumers should make shopping lists based on their weekly meal plans and check what they already have at home before making purchases. This would help them avoid unnecessary spending as well as reduce waste. Businesses also have an important role to play. Restaurants could offer smaller portions and encourage customers to take leftovers home. Supermarkets could sell products that are approaching their expiry dates at reduced prices and donate suitable unsold food to charities. Furthermore, governments could launch public awareness campaigns to explain the environmental and economic consequences of food waste, encouraging people to change their daily habits.

In conclusion, excessive purchasing and inappropriate portion sizes are two major causes of food waste. By improving consumer habits and encouraging more responsible business practices, society can significantly reduce the amount of food that is thrown away.

结构： 概括原因与对策 → 消费者及商家的原因 → 个人、企业、政府的解决方案 → 总结。

积累： go bad 变质；in advance 提前；perfectly edible food 完全可食用的食物；take leftovers home 打包剩菜；raise public awareness 提高公众意识；at reduced prices 以折扣价。

逐段拆解 · 逻辑与句子功能

P1 · Introduction（现象 + 概括原因和对策）

① Food waste has become ... — 改写问题；② This problem is largely caused by ... — 同时预告消费习惯、商业行为和解决措施，回应题目两个问题。

P2 · Body 1（分析原因）

① One reason ... — 购买过量；② Supermarkets frequently ... — 解释促销如何造成过量购买和变质；③ In addition ... — 补充未提前规划；④ Restaurants can also ... — 引入餐厅份量问题；⑤ As a result ... — 汇总后果。逻辑：原因 → 机制 → 另一原因 → 结果。

P3 · Body 2（提出对应对策）

① There are several ways ... — 总起；② On an individual level ... — 个人列购物清单，对应过量购买；③ This would help ... — 解释效果；④ Businesses also ... — 转向企业；⑤ Restaurants could ... — 小份餐和打包，对应份量过大；⑥ Supermarkets could ... — 临期折扣和捐赠；⑦ Furthermore, governments could ... — 政府宣传，改变习惯。逻辑：谁 → 做什么 → 为什么有效。

P4 · Conclusion（总结两问）

① In conclusion ... — 重申两个主要原因；② By improving ... — 总结解决路径与预期效果，不加入新措施。

可替换句型 · 绿色部分按题目更换

保留句法框架，替换绿色槽位；注意主谓一致、单复数及词性。例句不是万能套话。

开头：描述问题

[问题] has become a serious problem in many societies.

换题示例： Traffic congestion has become a serious problem in many cities.

交通拥堵已成为许多城市的严重问题。复数主语用 have。
开头：概括原因与对策

This problem is largely caused by [原因 A] and [原因 B], but several practical measures could help reduce it.

换题示例： This problem is largely caused by population growth and inadequate public transport, but several practical measures could help reduce it.

这个问题主要由人口增长和公共交通不足造成，但一些实际措施能缓解它。
原因：主题句

One reason why [现象/完整句子] is that [原因/完整句子].

换题示例： One reason why young people exercise less is that they spend too much time on electronic devices.

年轻人锻炼较少的一个原因是他们在电子设备上花费过多时间。
原因：行为与后果

[主体] can also contribute to the problem by [动名词].

换题示例： Employers can also contribute to the problem by requiring staff to work long hours.

雇主要求员工长时间工作也可能加剧问题。
结果：因果衔接

As a result, [完整结果] simply because [完整原因].

换题示例： As a result, many people become physically inactive simply because they have little free time.

因此，许多人仅仅因为缺乏空闲时间而变得缺少运动。
对策：个人层面

On an individual level, [人群] should [动词原形].

换题示例： On an individual level, commuters should use public transport whenever possible.

个人层面，通勤者应尽可能使用公共交通。
对策：解释效果

This would help [对象] [动词原形] as well as [动词原形].

换题示例： This would help residents save money as well as reduce air pollution.

这既能帮助居民省钱，也能减少空气污染。两处动词形式平行。
对策：政府层面

Governments could [措施], encouraging [人群] to [动词原形].

换题示例： Governments could improve cycling infrastructure, encouraging residents to cycle to work.

政府可以改善自行车基础设施，鼓励居民骑车上班。
结尾：手段 + 结果

By [动名词短语] and [动名词短语], society can [动词原形].

换题示例： By improving public transport and encouraging flexible working hours, society can reduce traffic congestion.

通过改善公共交通和鼓励弹性工作，社会可以减少交通拥堵。
